RESEARCH QUESTIONS → ANSWERS

The project should answer questions, not just show metrics.

01

Which model wins?

Selected by the predeclared rule (highest recall at FPR ≤ 10%) on verified local reproduction. In the measured primary experiment, Logistic Regression provided the best qualifying trade-off under the predefined ≤10% false-alarm constraint.

02

Why not use accuracy?

A detector can look "accurate" while still missing attacks or constantly flagging benign traffic. Detection and false alarms tell a clearer story.

03

Why temporal testing?

Because later traffic can look different. The temporal holdout asks whether the model still works when patterns shift over time.

04

Why synthetic data?

The same declared seed recreates the same study, keeping the benchmark self-contained, reproducible, and easy to audit.
